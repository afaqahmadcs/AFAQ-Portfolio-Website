About Me

Building modern digital experiences through thoughtful engineering and continuous learning.

Hi, I'm Afaq Ahmad

I'm an AI & Full Stack Developer based in Peshawar, Pakistan, currently pursuing FSc Computer Science at The Superior Group of Colleges. I enjoy building modern websites and exploring the possibilities of artificial intelligence to create digital solutions that are both practical and user-friendly.

My journey into technology officially began on 14 April 2026 when I joined the SMIT Web & App Development program. No single person inspired me to become a developer—I simply had a genuine curiosity about how websites and software are built. At the beginning, I didn't own a laptop, so I learned programming and wrote my first lines of code on my Android phone. After three to four months, I was able to buy my first laptop, which opened new opportunities to build better projects and continue learning. Living in a village has also meant dealing with internet connectivity issues and limited access to modern technology and development tools, but those challenges motivated me to keep improving instead of giving up.

My first major project was my own portfolio website, followed by several business websites that helped me gain practical experience and confidence as a developer. Looking ahead, my goal is to continue my education in Germany, graduate from a leading university, and build a successful career in software engineering and artificial intelligence. Whether I join an innovative technology company or build my own startup, I want to create products that solve real-world problems, improve people's lives, and continue learning every step of the way.

What I Stand For

Personal Values

Continuous Learning

Technology evolves fast. Staying current is not optional — it's the baseline expectation of any serious engineer.

Clean Code

Code is read far more than it is written. Clarity, naming, and structure matter as much as functionality.

Accessibility

The web should work for everyone. Accessibility is not a checklist — it's a fundamental design principle.

Performance

Every millisecond matters. Fast software is respectful software — it values the user's time and device.

Problem Solving

The best solutions come from deeply understanding the problem first, not rushing to implement the first idea.

User Experience

The measure of good design is how it feels to the person using it. Engineering without empathy misses the point.
