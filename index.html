Workflow

How I Approach Projects

A straightforward process focused on clear communication, responsive testing, and clean delivery.

Step 01
💬

Understanding

Clarifying what the website needs to achieve, what content is required, and who will visit it.

Step 02
📐

Structure

Planning the layout, content hierarchy, and responsive breakpoints before writing code.

Step 03
💻

Development

Writing semantic HTML structure, modular CSS, and purposeful JavaScript without unnecessary bloat.

Step 04
📱

Device Testing

Testing thoroughly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop viewports to ensure layout stability.

Step 05
🚀

Launch

Deploying to GitHub Pages, Vercel, or custom hosting with verified links and clean handoff.
