Background & Approach

From Academic Foundations to Real-World Shipping

A transparent look at how I learn, build, and work through problems.

My journey in web development began with writing basic HTML on a smartphone code editor before saving up to purchase my own laptop. That constraint taught me early on to appreciate clean structure and efficient code over flashy shortcuts.

Over the past six months at Saylani Mass IT Training (SMIT), I have paired daily practice with structured coursework—progressing from styling layouts with CSS Grid to handling application state and API requests in vanilla JavaScript. Building the client website for Blue Sapphire Gem Stones grounded that learning into real business needs: managing image assets, creating clean product displays, and routing customer leads directly to WhatsApp.

Studying Computer Science at Superior College complements my coding with theoretical foundations in algorithms and logic. I am currently beginning backend studies to understand server-side architecture and data flow beyond the client.
