Common Questions

Clear answers on availability, communication, and project process.

I build responsive landing pages, business showcase sites, product catalogs, and portfolio layouts using clean HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. I also help businesses fix broken mobile layouts, improve loading stability, and convert Figma wireframes into working code. I am equally open to junior frontend developer and internship opportunities.
I coordinate remotely using email, WhatsApp for quick updates, and GitHub for version-controlled code review. For the Blue Sapphire project, all requirements, revisions, and Vercel deployments were managed cleanly through remote communication. I schedule regular milestone check-ins so clients always know what has been built.
A single-page showcase or portfolio usually takes 1 to 2 weeks once content and requirements are clear. A structured multi-page business website typically takes 2 to 4 weeks. Before starting any work, I provide an honest delivery schedule with clear milestones to avoid missed deadlines.
For production client sites, I use semantic HTML5, modern CSS (Flexbox, CSS Grid, and custom property design tokens), and vanilla ES6+ JavaScript. I deploy projects to Vercel or GitHub Pages. In my coursework at SMIT, I am actively studying Node.js, Express, and MongoDB to understand full-stack data flow.
