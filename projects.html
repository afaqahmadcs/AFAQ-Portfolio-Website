Blue Sapphire Gem Stones

An active commercial showcase website developed for a gemstone business in Peshawar, presenting certified natural sapphires and routing customer inquiries directly into private WhatsApp sales conversations.

Role & Contribution Frontend Architecture & UI
Intended Audience Collectors & Luxury Buyers
Core Stack HTML5, Modern CSS3, Vanilla JS, Vercel
01 · The Problem & Business Need

High-value natural gemstones cannot be sold like conventional commodities; buyers need stone provenance, carat weight, cut accuracy, and lab certification details before initiating contact. The business lacked an organized digital presence to display inventory and instead handled inquiries through scattered photos.

02 · Approach & Implementation

Implemented a dark luxury aesthetic (sapphire navy with warm gold accents) that emphasizes gemstone color. Designed responsive inventory cards displaying carat, cut, clarity, and origin, paired with one-click WhatsApp routing that pre-populates the specific gemstone reference in the conversation.

03 · What Was Built (Verified Features)

Live catalog with responsive image optimization, mobile-friendly navigation, modal stone specifications, and WhatsApp lead routing. Deployed and verified on Vercel with HTTPS and zero console errors.

04 · Measurable Outcome & Reflection

Delivered a live commercial production website running on Vercel that actively routes qualified buyer leads directly into WhatsApp. Working directly with a business owner reinforced the power of ruthless prioritization: replacing an unneeded complex checkout cart with direct WhatsApp communication matched the actual buying behavior of high-ticket gem clients.

Afaq Ahmad Real Estate

A multi-page property advisory concept exploring complex document architecture, property parameter cards, and persistent theme toggling for luxury real estate.

Role & Contribution Full Design & Frontend Code
Intended Audience Luxury Property Investors
Core Stack Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
01 · Design Goal & Challenge

Real estate interfaces often break down when displaying multiple parameters (beds, baths, square footage, price, location badges) across small screens. The goal was to build a clean editorial hierarchy with modular CSS variables across 9 structured pages.

02 · Approach & Implementation

Separated CSS into decoupled modules (`layout.css`, `cards.css`, `forms.css`, `variables.css`). Built property cards using CSS Grid with auto-fit columns and added local storage-backed dark/light theme switching.

03 · What This Demonstrates

Demonstrates multi-page routing structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, client-side consultation booking forms, and responsive property metadata cards without framework overhead.

04 · Measurable Outcome & Reflection

Architected and launched a 100% framework-free, 9-page architecture on GitHub Pages with zero CSS regressions. Proved that organized design tokens and semantic HTML can deliver a polished, accessible luxury real estate experience with sub-second page rendering and zero layout shift.
